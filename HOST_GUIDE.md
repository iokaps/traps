# 🎮 Traps Host Guide - Let's Get This Party Started!

## Who's This For? 🤔

**You are the perfect host if:**
- You're planning a **game night with friends** (2-8 people)
- You want something **fun and interactive** that doesn't require preparation
- You've got a **laptop/desktop** to control the action
- You have a **big screen** nearby (TV, projector) for spectators to hype up the players *(optional but way more fun!)*

**You're NOT the host for:**
- Classroom/educational settings *(Teachers, this ain't for you... yet!)*
- Solo players *(This is a social game - you need at least 2 friends to throw traps at!)*

- 🍕 **Friday game nights** - More fun than just scrolling

## How to Run It 🚀

### Step 1: Get Everyone's Phone Ready
Tell your friends to **open the game link on their phones** (you'll share this with them once you're ready). They should be on **phones or tablets** - that's where the gameplay happens.

> **Tip:** Make sure they're on the same WiFi as you, or they'll have a bad time connecting!

### Step 2: Open It on Your Desktop 💻
- Open the **Traps app on your laptop or desktop** (the big screen)
- You'll see a host setup screen
- Take a deep breath - you're about to configure the game!

### Step 3: Configure Your Game 🎛️

When you open the host screen, you'll see these settings:

**⏱️ Number of Rounds**
- How many questions do you want to play?
- *Suggested: 3-5 rounds* for a casual night *(takes about 10-15 min)*
- *Suggested: 1-2 rounds* if people are impatient 😅

**⏰ Question Time**
- How long should players have to answer each question?
- *Suggested: 15 seconds* (default) - keeps things snappy
- *Suggested: 20 seconds* if you want to be nice
- *Suggested: 10 seconds* if you want absolute chaos and tears

> **Pro tip:** Shorter time = more points for speed, more drama, more fun!

### Step 4: Generate Links (Share the Chaos) 🔗

Once you've configured everything, you'll get:
- 🎮 **Player Link** - Share this with everyone joining to play
- 📺 **Presenter Link** *(optional)* - Use this on a big screen for spectators to watch

**How to share:**
- Copy the link and send it via text/WhatsApp
- Or use the **QR code** and let them scan
- Or just tell them verbally and let them fumble for it 😄

### Step 5: Wait for Players to Join ⏳

- Players open the link and enter their **names**
- You'll see them appear on your host screen as they join
- Wait for at least **2 players** (minimum to start)
- Once everyone's ready and you see them listed... *it's go time!*

### Step 6: Press "Start Game" 🎬

When everyone's loaded their profiles:
1. Make sure all your players have phones ready
2. Click **"Start Game"** on your screen
3. Watch the magic happen:
   - ✨ **Get Ready phase** - 5 second countdown (get hyped!)
   - 🗳️ **Category Vote** - Players vote on quiz topic
   - 💣 **Trap Selection** - Players choose traps to throw at each other *(this is where the trash talk starts)*
   - ❓ **Question** - AI-generated trivia appears
   - 📊 **Results** - Points awarded, leaderboard updated
   - 🔄 **Repeat** - Until you hit the round limit

### Step 7: Watch the Chaos Unfold 👀

Your job now:
- **Monitor the action** on your screen - you're the "game master"
- **Keep score** mentally - or let the game do it for you
- **Hype up the big plays** - when someone clutches a question or lands a perfect trap
- **Call out the betrayals** - someone threw a mud trap at their best friend? *Noice!*

## What Hosts Actually Control During Game 🎮

**Before the game starts:**
- ✅ Configure round count and question time
- ✅ Share links with players
- ✅ Start the game when everyone's ready

**During the game:**
- 👀 Just sit back and watch your host screen update in real-time
- 📱 The game controls itself - moves to next phase automatically
- 🎙️ You're basically a commentator now!

**The game flows automatically:**
- You DON'T manually ask questions (AI handles it ✨)
- You DON'T score manually (game calculates it 🤖)
- You DON'T pick the category (players vote 🗳️)
- You just... host the vibes!

---

## Scoring & How Players Win 🏆

**How points work:**
- Correct answer = **100 points** (base)
- **Speed bonus** = up to 900 extra points *(answer faster = more money)*
  - Fastest answer = ~900 bonus points
  - Slow answer = ~0 bonus points

**Example:** Dream answers their trivia in 2 seconds → **~1000 points** 😎

**The traps add chaos:**
- When someone throws a trap at a player, that player has to **clear the trap BEFORE answering**
- More traps = harder to answer fast = fewer points (or wrong answer 💥)
- This is how weaker players can take down the leaders!

**Who wins:** The player with the **highest score after all rounds** is the CHAMPION! 🎊

1. **Test the WiFi first** - Nothing kills the vibe like connection issues

## Troubleshooting 🔧

**"Players can't see the game?"**
- Make sure they opened the link correctly
- They should see a name entry screen, then "Waiting for game to start"
- If not, check WiFi connection

**"The game won't start?"**
- Make sure at least 2 players have joined
- Click "Start Game" on YOUR host screen (not their phones!)

**"Someone got disconnected?"**
- They're out for that round (game skips offline players)
- Their phone will try to reconnect automatically
- If they refresh their link, they can rejoin (but they lost points that round 😬)

**"The timer seems fast?"**
- Yep, that's intentional! Keeps the adrenaline up
- You configured it earlier - remember? 😄

## The Fun Bit - The Traps! 💣

Players will throw **4 types of traps** at each other:

- 🧊 **Ice Trap** - Freezes your screen briefly
- 🪨 **Mud Trap** - Slows down your response time
- 🔥 **Fire Trap** - Something spicy happens
- 💨 **Wind Trap** - Blows things around

Players must **clear any traps before answering** - good luck if you got hit with 3 mud traps from your enemies! 😆
